Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Application Tracker (applicationtracker.tech) is a small personal project run by Braydon Callender. It helps you keep track of your own job applications. This page explains what it stores and what it does with it.

What is collected

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account ID, email address, and name. The app asks Google only for the basic openid, email, and profile scopes. It does not get your Google password and cannot access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter: applications, job descriptions, notes, interview entries, and files you upload.
  • Sign-in session: a cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Only a hash of it is stored on the server. No advertising or analytics cookies are used.
  • Server logs: the web server records requests (IP address, time, page, browser type) for security and troubleshooting. Logs are deleted after 30 days.

How it is used

Your data is used only to show your tracker to you. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Other users cannot see your data.

When you use "Fill in from link", the server downloads that one job page to read the job details. That request comes from the server, not your browser, and the page's site sees the server's address.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database and a file folder on a private server (a virtual private server hosted by Hostinger). Connections use HTTPS. Uploaded images have their metadata (such as location data) removed. Encrypted-at-rest guarantees depend on the hosting provider. Backups are made nightly and kept for 14 days.

Your choices

  • Export: download all of your applications as a CSV file from the Account page at any time.
  • Delete: "Delete my account" on the Account page permanently removes your account, all applications, interviews, and files right away. Copies in backups expire within 14 days.
  • You can also remove the app's access in your Google account settings under Security → Third-party apps.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
